The Volkswagen T-Cross (Facelifted Model - Post 2024 - HP Figures)
The Volkswagen T-Cross is a popular compact SUV (B-SUV segment) that has carved out a strong niche in the market since its launch in 2019. It's built on the same MQB A0 platform as the Polo, offering a raised driving position and a more rugged appearance while retaining the agility of a smaller car. The T-Cross received a significant facelift in 2024 (referring to current models in 2025, considering the recent updates), bringing it up to date with Volkswagen's latest design and technology.

Key Features and Technology
The recent facelift introduced several key improvements to the T-Cross:

Refreshed Exterior Design: The front and rear have been updated with new bumpers and a revised light signature. Standard LED headlights and taillights are now included across the range, with higher trims featuring IQ.LIGHT Matrix LED headlights and a distinctive LED light bar connecting the headlights and taillights, providing a more modern and cohesive look, similar to other new VW models.

Enhanced Interior Quality: While still a compact SUV, Volkswagen has focused on improving the perceived quality of the interior, with softer touch materials in key areas and a more refined finish.

Digital Cockpit: The T-Cross now comes with a Digital Cockpit as standard, typically an 8-inch screen, which can be upgraded to a larger Digital Cockpit Pro (around 10 inches) on higher trim levels. This provides a customizable digital instrument display.

Updated Infotainment System: The central infotainment screen is larger, typically 8 inches as standard, with a 9.2-inch touchscreen available on top trims. It includes features like Wireless App-Connect for Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, USB-C ports (including in the rear), and enhanced connectivity services.

Sliding Rear Bench: A standout feature for practicality, the rear bench can slide longitudinally by 140 mm. This allows users to prioritize either legroom for passengers or boot space, making it highly versatile for different needs. The front passenger seat can also be folded flat (standard from the Urban version onwards in Portugal), allowing for objects almost 2.4 meters long to be carried.

Advanced Driver Assistance Systems: The T-Cross incorporates a range of safety and assistance technologies:

Travel Assist: Combining Adaptive Cruise Control and Lane Assist, this system enables semi-automated driving, offering greater comfort and safety on long journeys.

Front Assist with autonomous emergency braking and pedestrian/cyclist monitoring.

Traffic Sign Recognition.

Park Assist: For automated parking maneuvers.

Rear Parking Sensors are standard even on the entry-level.

Engine Options
In Portugal and many other European markets, the T-Cross primarily focuses on petrol engines:

1.0 TSI (3-cylinder turbocharged petrol):

94 HP: This is the entry-level option, typically paired with a 5-speed manual gearbox. It's well-suited for urban driving and provides good fuel efficiency.

114 HP: A more powerful version, available with either a 6-speed manual gearbox or a 7-speed DSG automatic transmission. This engine offers a better balance of performance for both city and highway driving.

1.5 TSI (4-cylinder turbocharged petrol):

While less common in all markets for the T-Cross, a 148 HP 1.5-liter TSI engine paired with a DSG gearbox was available in some regions, offering stronger performance. However, recent information for Portugal indicates a primary focus on the 1.0 TSI variants.

Diesel engines are generally no longer offered for the T-Cross in new vehicle sales in Europe.

Trim Levels (in Portugal)
In Portugal, the T-Cross is typically offered with a clear hierarchy of trim levels, often including:

T-Cross (Base/Entry-level): Even this initial trim comes well-equipped with LED headlights, LED taillights, traffic sign recognition, 8-inch Digital Cockpit, 8-inch infotainment system, USB-C ports, and rear parking sensors.

Urban: This is often the more popular mid-range trim, adding features like 17-inch alloy wheels, and potentially further technology and comfort enhancements.

Style: Positioned as a more elegant and feature-rich trim, offering premium touches, improved interior materials, and a more comprehensive set of technological features (e.g., larger infotainment, Digital Cockpit Pro, ambient lighting).

R-Line: This trim emphasizes sportiness, featuring unique R-Line exterior styling elements (sportier bumpers, larger wheels), sports seats in the interior, and specific R-Line badging.

Dimensions and Practicality
The T-Cross is designed to be a compact SUV that maximizes interior space.

Length: Approximately 4,135 mm (after facelift, slightly longer than previous)

Width: Approximately 1,760 mm (excluding mirrors)

Height: Approximately 1,559 mm

Wheelbase: Approximately 2,551 mm

Boot Space: A highly flexible boot ranging from 385 liters to 455 liters depending on the position of the sliding rear bench. With the rear seats folded down, the capacity can expand significantly to up to 1,281 liters, making it very practical for its segment.

The Volkswagen T-Cross remains a strong competitor in the highly contested B-SUV segment, offering a compelling blend of contemporary design, advanced technology, flexible interior space, and the renowned Volkswagen build quality.
